# Immersive Theater Tokyo 🎭

A browser-based spy platformer, made as a birthday gift.

You play the Nightingale, an elite spy inside the Immersive Theater Tokyo.
Move through the grand hall, backstage rigging, and the lighting catwalk.
Take down the guards — each carries a torn piece of a photograph. Collect
every piece, tape the photo back together at each locked door to crack its
riddle, then face The Impresario for the final act. Climb the ropes and lob
bombs to clear your path — and enjoy the surprise at the end.

## Play
It's a fully static site — no server or database needed. Just open
`index.html`, or visit the hosted link (GitHub Pages).

## Controls
- Move: **A / D** or **← / →**
- Jump: **W / ↑ / Space**
- Climb ropes: **W / S**
- Shoot: **J / F**
- Throw bomb: **K / B**
- Mute: **M** (or the 🔊 button)

On phones/tablets, on-screen touch buttons appear automatically. Play in
landscape for the best fit.

## Files
- `index.html`, `style.css`, `game.js` — the game
- `images/` — clue photos (lantern, mask, key)
